# Memory: Do not treat EU rhetoric as law

## Trigger
EU agenda items involving Commission statements, Parliament resolutions, Council debates, summits, AI Act, DSA, DMA, sanctions, trade defense, climate policy, enlargement, or industrial policy.

## Pattern
Agent treats political language or institutional intent as if it already created binding obligations.

## Better reasoning
First identify the institutional path: Commission proposal, Council mandate, Parliament position, trilogue, delegated/implementing act, agency guidance, CJEU judgment, or national enforcement. Legal weight depends on the stage.

## Apply when
An EU development could affect market access, compliance, sanctions, digital rules, climate rules, competition, or trade.

## Do not apply when
The item is already a final legal act, binding guidance, court judgment, or enforcement action.

## Watch indicators
Legal text, Council mandate, Parliament vote, trilogue agreement, delegated act, agency guidance, compliance deadline, national regulator action, fine, or CJEU judgment.

## Example rewrite
Before: “The EU has changed the rules for companies.”

After: “This is a compliance-relevant signal, but the obligation depends on whether it becomes legal text, delegated guidance, or enforcement practice.”
