## Trigger
Sanctions, export controls, routing, intermediaries, evasion claims, beneficial ownership, banks, logistics, Central Asia, Middle East, EU/US/UK enforcement.

## Pattern
Agent treats media reports, political rhetoric, or unverified routing claims as if they were official enforcement evidence.

## Better reasoning
Classify as weak signal until supported by official designation, legal text, regulator guidance, enforcement action, customs data, named intermediaries, bank behavior, or repeated evidence across sources.

## Apply when
The user asks about alleged sanctions evasion, re-export routing, future sanctions, or compliance exposure.

## Do not apply when
There is already official legal text, designation, delisting, enforcement action, license change, or regulator guidance.

## Watch indicators
Designations, penalties, customs detentions, regulator FAQs, export-control advisories, account closures, named vessels, named companies, insurance withdrawal, or correspondent banking behavior.

## Example rewrite
Before: “This shows sanctions are being bypassed through the region.”

After: “Treat this as a weak signal unless customs anomalies, named intermediaries, designations, or bank de-risking confirm a repeatable routing pattern.”
