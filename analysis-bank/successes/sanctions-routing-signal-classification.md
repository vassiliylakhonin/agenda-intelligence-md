# Memory: Classify sanctions routing through mechanisms, not geography

## Trigger
Sanctions routing, re-export, dual-use goods, Central Asia, Middle Corridor, UAE/Turkey/Caucasus, logistics hubs, banks, customs, intermediaries.

## Pattern
Strong outputs avoid treating a region as a passive route and instead identify the mechanisms that create exposure.

## Better reasoning
Map the exposure mechanism: goods, payment chain, logistics documentation, beneficial ownership, customs classification, insurance, trade finance, or facilitation. Then classify signal strength based on evidence quality.

## Apply when
A user asks whether trade flows, routing, or intermediaries create sanctions/compliance risk.

## Do not apply when
The issue is purely diplomatic and has no goods, payments, logistics, ownership, or enforcement mechanism.

## Watch indicators
HS-code anomalies, named intermediaries, bank guidance, account closures, designations, customs inspections, export-control advisories, insurance withdrawal, and route redesign.

## Example rewrite
Before: “Central Asia is becoming a sanctions bypass route.”

After: “The risk concentrates in intermediaries, customs documentation, banks, and dual-use goods. Upgrade from weak signal only if official actions or repeated trade-data anomalies support the claim.”
