# AI Vendor Evidence-Readiness

AI vendor evidence-readiness is the current commercial discovery wedge for Agenda Intelligence MD.

The workflow turns a public or redacted source pack into a human-review packet showing:

- buyer questions implied by an RFP, vendor packet, model card, or trust page;
- vendor or system claims;
- evidence present, weak, missing, or not assessable;
- reviewer owner actions;
- readiness route before shortlist, approval, escalation, renewal, or committee review.

## Buyer hypothesis

The economic buyer is a regulated procurement owner, AI governance owner, vendor-risk lead, public-sector CIO / Chief AI Officer, or delegated assurance owner who can approve review time, data access, operational adoption, or a paid concierge review.

The painful trigger is an active AI vendor review where the evidence packet is scattered across RFP answers, vendor pages, trust/security docs, policy claims, model cards, and standards mappings.

## Current workaround

Teams use RFP scoring sheets, vendor questionnaires, spreadsheets, GRC workflows, security/privacy review, legal review, committee memos, consultants, and ad hoc governance meetings.

## Boundary

This is not vendor approval, compliance advice, legal advice, security certification, procurement scoring, factual-truth verification, or autonomous decision-making. The output routes evidence gaps to humans.

## Success signal

Success is buyer behavior: a redacted file, second profile request, paid concierge review interest, budget-owner introduction, or concrete workflow correction.

Traffic, citations, likes, and "interesting" feedback are not traction.
