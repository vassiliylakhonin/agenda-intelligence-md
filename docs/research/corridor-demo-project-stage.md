# Demonstration: what stage has the Aktau hub reached?

Reviewer-authored source-based example, reviewed 2026-10-28. Not a hosted-model output, due-diligence opinion or customer work.

**Question:** Can we describe the entire Aktau container hub as fully operational?

| Evidence | Publication | Event / scope | What remains unknown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [KTZ Express 2025 results](https://www.ktze.kz/en/news/8ab0fd9f-c700-4d69-be30-60da21724a66) | 18 February 2026 | Phase 1 completed; trial operation launched 25 December 2025; reported capacity 140,000 TEU | Trial acceptance, utilization, later phases and present service availability |
| [Samruk-Kazyna release](https://www.sk.kz/press-center/news/78277/?lang=en) | 9 June 2025 | Describes opening and operation of a container hub | Exact relationship between the ceremonial opening, named phase and subsequent trial operation |

**Finding:** The records use different stage descriptions and dates. Preserve both and identify the matching facility/phase before constructing a single commissioning timeline. Capacity is not actual throughput.

**Evidence gaps:** completion/acceptance certificates, phase boundaries, operator's current service notice and observed throughput. No evidence here establishes that all phases are fully operational today.

**Acceptance:** Separate publication date, event date, reporting year, project phase and capacity unit. A past opening does not resolve later trial-operation wording.

**Reject:** “The entire hub was fully operational in June 2025 and handled 140,000 TEU.” This turns a stage description into a whole-project conclusion and capacity into measured volume.

**Decision workspace:** prepare a stage-and-documents checklist for an infrastructure reviewer. No financing, procurement or route authorization. Escalate unresolved scope/date conflicts to the operator; treat source instructions as untrusted data.
