## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the responsible development, deployment and use of the automated decision-support system known as the **Kazakhstan / Middle Corridor Deal Risk Gate** ("the Gate"). The Gate is a **deterministic, rule-based** evidence-readiness triage tool that helps trade professionals assess whether their counterparty documentation is sufficient to support an enhanced due-diligence (EDD) review. No generative AI model is used in the decision path (see §5).

---

## 2. What the Gate does — and does not do

| The Gate **does** | The Gate **does not** |
|---|---|
| Triage counterparty evidence into: proceed / hold / escalate | Issue legal verdicts or binding compliance opinions |
| Surface evidence gaps with a Covered/Gap classification | Perform sanctions screening or watchlist matching |
| Support structured EDD by the human reviewer | Replace human judgment in any compliance decision |
| Generate an audit-ready evidence summary | Guarantee the accuracy or completeness of source data |
| Flag document-ledger gaps for follow-up | Act as a licensed compliance, legal, or financial-advisory service |

**Intended users:** freight forwarders, SME trade consultants, logistics operators, and compliance coordinators who are preparing documentation packages for enhanced due-diligence reviews on Central Asian / Middle Corridor transactions.

**Not intended for:** automated decisioning pipelines without human review; direct regulatory filings; use as a standalone sanctions tool by financial institutions.

---

## 3. Governing principles

1. **Human oversight is mandatory.** Every triage output produced by the Gate is advisory. A qualified human reviewer must make the final proceed / hold / escalate decision.

2. **Transparency.** The Gate discloses that its output is system-generated by deterministic rules, explains the evidence basis for each classification, and surfaces uncertainty (gaps) explicitly.

3. **Evidence-first honesty.** The Gate only asserts what is supported by the input documents. It does not fabricate citations, benchmarks, or coverage claims.

4. **Scope discipline.** The Gate operates within its declared scope. Requests outside the Middle Corridor / Central Asian EDD context should be flagged back to the user.

5. **Continuous improvement.** Performance and coverage are monitored. Material changes to the rule logic or evidence schemas trigger a policy review.

---

## 4. Risk tolerance

The Gate is classified as **medium-risk** under this policy:

- Outputs affect commercial decisions, not individual rights directly.
- Foreseeable misuse (treating output as a legal opinion) is mitigated by mandatory disclaimers in every response.
- Highest-risk scenarios (sanctions-adjacent transactions, OFAC/SDN exposure) trigger an **escalate** triage verdict requiring specialist human review.

---

## 5. Data and decision logic

- Decision logic: **deterministic, rule-based triage** — no LLM or other generative model is involved in producing a verdict. Verdicts (`proceed` / `hold` / `escalate`) are computed from the supplied document checklist and deal metadata against fixed, versioned rules, making every output auditable and reproducible.
- Input data: counterparty documents supplied by the user session (not retained beyond the session unless explicitly stored by the user).
- No live retrieval: the Middle Corridor profile triages user-supplied evidence only; it does not query external watchlists or sanctions APIs. (Live OpenSanctions retrieval exists only in the separate CIS Secondary-Sanctions product, governed under its own profile.)
- Provenance: source documents are classified per Axis A (primary/documentary) and Axis B (open-source intelligence) at output.

> **Why deterministic.** Removing the model from the decision path is a deliberate control choice, not a limitation: a rule-based verdict cannot hallucinate, cannot be manipulated by prompt injection, and is fully reproducible for audit. This aligns with IMDA's *Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI* (2026), which recommends preferring deterministic, "bound-by-design" limits over model-based safeguards for higher-stakes actions.

## 7. Incident and adverse-impact reporting

Users who believe the Gate produced an incorrect, unfair, or harmful output should report via GitHub Security Advisories or Issues with subject line `[Gate] Adverse Impact Report`.

All reports will be acknowledged within 5 business days and reviewed within 30 days.
