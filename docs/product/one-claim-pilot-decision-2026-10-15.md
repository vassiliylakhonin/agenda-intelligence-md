# One-claim citation-review pilot decision

- goal: make one real claim/source/quote review usable without editing identifiers or JSON; prepare a self-serve pilot while no participants are available.
- trusted_evidence: four candidate completions on 12 October; no independently established usefulness or willingness to pay. The current form requires nested claim/source/quote records. The owner requested autonomous preparation and confirmed there are no participants.
- suspected_unreliable_evidence: discovery traffic and owner practice do not establish demand. Exact quote presence does not establish semantic support. A successful self-test is not a recruited session.
- hidden_assumptions: a narrower one-claim entry helps users try the existing service; this is a usability hypothesis, not a measured conversion improvement.
- intended_next_action: add a blank one-claim form that creates the existing request with explicit caller-selected support and verbatim source/quote; reuse trial, recovery and feedback. Prepare participant instructions and an empty session ledger. Exercise the bundled form and actual local trial handler on public documents, then release the Output Worker through Vizier.
- stop_or_escalate_if: input meaning is inferred, source/quote content is invented or fetched automatically, limits/payment are weakened, real users or feedback are fabricated, or publication/deployment gates fail. No external invitations are sent.

Missing human participation remains a real limit. Self-tests are excluded from
the participant ledger and cannot satisfy retention or payment criteria.

Only the short-form submission carries a bounded, unverified review-flow claim;
advanced-editor calls do not inherit it. No source text, arbitrary header or
new browser identifier is added to telemetry.
