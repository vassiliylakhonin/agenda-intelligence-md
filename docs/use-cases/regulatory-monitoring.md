# Use‑case: Regulatory Monitoring (policy tracking)

**Audience:** Policy analysts, compliance teams, regulators’ support staff.

**Goal:** Monitor evolving regulations and legislation that directly impact specific industries or activities.

**How Agenda‑Intelligence helps:**
- Uses source plans to state which regulatory documents, notices, and supporting sources should be checked by the analyst or upstream retrieval stack.
- Generates concise, decision‑ready briefs that indicate the regulatory change, its implications, affected stakeholders, and which indicators to track next.
- Validation checks structure, while scoring gives a heuristic view of evidence discipline and decision-readiness signals.

---

**Typical workflow (condensed):**
1. Run `agenda-intelligence start regulation`.
2. Fill the brief template with the newly identified regulatory change.
3. Validate with `validate‑brief`.
4. Score the brief.
5. Distribute to stakeholders.

---

**Result:** Teams get a repeatable contract for turning externally collected regulatory updates into evidence-linked briefs, without treating schema validation as factual verification.
