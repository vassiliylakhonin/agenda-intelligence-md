# Use‑case: Founder Operating Context

**Audience:** Startup founders, COOs, product leaders, board members.

**Goal:** Stay on top of macro‑level changes—regulatory, geopolitical, supply‑chain, talent‑market—that affect company strategy.

**How Agenda‑Intelligence helps:**
- Provides a structured way to digest complex external signals without drowning in newsletters.
- Provides one‑page brief contracts that highlight *what changed*, *why it matters*, *who is affected*, and *what to watch next*.
- Evidence‑mode forces the founder to distinguish checked sources, user-provided evidence, inference, and unsupported claims.

**Typical workflow:**
1. Pick a relevant source‑plan (e.g., `technology‑ai`, `regional‑risk`, `trade`).
2. Run `agenda-intelligence start <plan>` to get a trimmed plan and brief template.
3. Fill the brief with an externally observed development (e.g., new AI regulation, shipping lane disruption).
4. Validate, diagnose source-plan coverage where relevant, and score the brief; share it with the leadership team or board.

---
**Result:** Founders get a repeatable, low‑noise operating cadence that turns external chaos into clear strategic inputs.
