# Agenda Triage

## Classify the development

Use one primary label:

- **Noise** — attention-heavy but decision-light.
- **Weak signal** — possibly relevant, insufficient confirmation.
- **Signal** — meaningful development that changes assessment or monitoring.
- **Structural shift** — durable change in incentives, constraints, rules, or power.
- **Trigger event** — development that starts or accelerates a concrete risk pathway.
- **Compliance-relevant development** — changes sanctions, export controls, legal/regulatory exposure, or enforcement risk.
- **Reputational-risk development** — changes legitimacy, public scrutiny, partner risk, or stakeholder expectations.

## Triage questions

Ask:

1. Is this new or just newly visible?
2. Does it change incentives or only rhetoric?
3. Does it alter rules, resources, capabilities, alliances, enforcement, or timing?
4. Who has to act differently because of it?
5. What would be observable if this is a real shift?
6. What would make this a false alarm?

## Escalation markers

Treat as higher-signal when the development includes:

- formal legal or regulatory change;
- budget/resource allocation;
- enforcement action;
- elite defection or coalition shift;
- sanctions designation or delisting;
- military movement or security incident;
- market access restriction;
- institutional coordination across states;
- credible implementation deadline;
- repeated signals from separate actors.

## Downgrade markers

Treat as lower-signal when the development is:

- symbolic rhetoric without implementation path;
- single-source rumor;
- recycled claim;
- domestic performance with no external mechanism;
- vague pledge without budget, authority, or timeline;
- already priced into prior assumptions.
