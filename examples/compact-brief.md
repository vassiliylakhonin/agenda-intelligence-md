# Example: Compact Brief

**Bottom line:** This is a signal, not yet a structural shift. It changes monitoring priority but does not justify a major posture change without implementation evidence.

**Signal classification:** Signal.

**What changed:** A relevant actor moved from vague rhetoric to a more specific policy position with a visible implementation path.

**Why it matters:** The development may shorten the timeline for regulatory exposure and increase partner due-diligence pressure.

**Who is affected:**
- regulated companies: higher compliance preparation burden;
- investors: greater policy-timing risk;
- counterparties: more scrutiny of exposure and documentation.

**Main uncertainty:** Whether the announcement becomes enforceable policy or remains political signaling.

**Scenarios:**
- Base case: slow implementation, rising monitoring cost.
- Downside: accelerated enforcement or broader scope.
- Upside: watered-down implementation after stakeholder pushback.

**Watch next:** draft text, enforcement agency statements, budget allocation, implementation deadline, first enforcement action.
