# China rare earth processing technology ban

**Signal classification:** escalation_marker
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

China bans export of rare earth magnet manufacturing technology, tightening control over the permanent magnet supply chain critical for EVs and wind turbines.

## What changed

China added rare earth separation, smelting, and magnet manufacturing technologies to the prohibited export catalog.

## Why it matters

Permanent magnets using NdFeB are essential for EV motors and wind turbines; technology ban prevents competitors from building independent processing capacity.

## Main uncertainty

Enforcement against technology transfer via joint ventures and personnel movement is difficult; existing licensed facilities outside China unaffected.

## Affected actors

- China MOFCOM
- EV manufacturers
- wind turbine manufacturers
- non-China rare earth miners
- industrial policy agencies (US DOE, EU)

## Scenarios

**Effective bottleneck:** Technology ban delays non-China capacity by 5+ years.
- No new magnet plants announced outside China
- price premium widens

**Workaround proliferation:** Alternative technologies or licensed pre-ban facilities fill gap.
- New magnet plants break ground
- ferrite or alternative motor designs gain adoption

## Watch next

- Technology transfer enforcement cases
- Non-China magnet production capacity announcements
- EV/wind OEM supply diversification strategies
