# Taiwan Strait military exercises

**Signal classification:** escalation_marker
**Evidence mode:** mixed

## Bottom line

PLA military exercises near Taiwan increase shipping insurance costs and supply chain risk for semiconductor-dependent industries.

## What changed

PLA announced large-scale military exercises in waters surrounding Taiwan, including live-fire drills in designated exclusion zones overlapping commercial shipping lanes.

## Why it matters

Taiwan Strait handles ~50% of global container traffic; exclusion zones disrupt shipping schedules and raise war-risk premiums.

## Main uncertainty

Exercise duration and whether exclusion zones will be enforced against commercial vessels remain unclear.

## Affected actors

- PLA
- Taiwan defense ministry
- container shipping lines
- semiconductor buyers
- war-risk insurers

## Scenarios

**Short exercise, no escalation:** Exercises end within announced period; shipping resumes normally.
- PLA withdraws from exclusion zones
- war-risk premiums normalize

**Extended presence:** PLA maintains naval presence beyond announced dates; shipping reroutes.
- Continued NOTAM/NOTMAR extensions
- shipping line schedule changes
- semiconductor lead time increases

## Watch next

- Exercise end date announcement
- War-risk premium changes from Lloyd's JWC
- TSMC supply chain advisories
