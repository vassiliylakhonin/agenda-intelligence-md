# Iran secondary sanctions expansion

**Signal classification:** enforcement_marker
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

OFAC expands secondary sanctions targeting Iran's petrochemical and shipping networks, increasing compliance risk for intermediaries.

## What changed

New OFAC designations added 30+ entities to the SDN list, including shipping companies, port operators, and petrochemical traders facilitating Iranian exports.

## Why it matters

Intermediaries in UAE, Turkey, and Southeast Asia face direct designation risk; banks processing related transactions face secondary sanctions exposure.

## Main uncertainty

Enforcement intensity against non-US intermediaries remains to be tested; some designated entities may reconstitute under new names.

## Affected actors

- OFAC
- designated entities
- UAE/Turkey commodity traders
- correspondent banks
- shipping insurers

## Scenarios

**Aggressive enforcement:** OFAC pursues secondary sanctions cases against non-US banks within 6 months.
- Settlement announcements
- compliance advisory updates

**Designation without follow-through:** Designations serve as deterrent but enforcement resources focus elsewhere.
- No new enforcement actions within 12 months
- designated entities continue operating

## Watch next

- OFAC enforcement actions against non-US persons
- De-listing petitions from designated entities
- P&I club withdrawal notices for flagged vessels
