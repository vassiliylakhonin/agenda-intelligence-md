# Hormuz Strait tanker incident

**Signal classification:** escalation_marker
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

Iranian seizure of a commercial tanker in the Strait of Hormuz escalates maritime risk, raising war-risk premiums and oil supply disruption probability.

## What changed

IRGC Navy seized a commercial tanker transiting the Strait of Hormuz; vessel flagged to a non-Iranian state; crew detained.

## Why it matters

Hormuz handles ~20% of global oil trade; tanker seizure signals willingness to escalate, affecting insurance costs and routing decisions.

## Main uncertainty

Whether seizure is isolated incident or start of a pattern; diplomatic resolution timeline unknown.

## Affected actors

- IRGC Navy
- flag state government
- P&I clubs
- oil traders
- US Fifth Fleet
- Lloyd's Joint War Committee

## Scenarios

**Diplomatic resolution:** Vessel and crew released after negotiations; incident treated as isolated.
- Release confirmed
- no JWC area change
- premiums normalize

**Pattern escalation:** Additional seizures or threats; Hormuz risk premium becomes structural.
- Second incident within 30 days
- JWC expands listed area
- shipping reroutes via Cape
