# EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive

**Signal classification:** compliance_relevant_development
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

CSRD mandates detailed ESG reporting for large EU and non-EU companies; first filings due in 2025 for FY2024, with phased extension to smaller firms.

## What changed

European Financial Reporting Advisory Group published final European Sustainability Reporting Standards; first wave of companies must comply.

## Why it matters

Affects ~50,000 companies including non-EU firms with significant EU revenue; requires audited sustainability data, double materiality assessment.

## Main uncertainty

Third-country equivalence for non-EU standards not yet decided; assurance standards for sustainability data still developing.

## Affected actors

- EFRAG
- large EU companies
- non-EU companies with EU revenue above threshold
- audit firms
- ESG data providers

## Scenarios

**Rapid convergence:** CSRD becomes de facto global standard via supply chain pressure.
- Non-EU jurisdictions adopt compatible frameworks
- investor demand for CSRD-format data

**Fragmented compliance:** Companies treat CSRD as box-ticking; data quality varies widely.
- High restatement rates
- enforcement actions for misleading reporting

## Watch next

- First CSRD filings quality and comparability
- Third-country equivalence decisions
- ESMA enforcement priorities for sustainability reporting
