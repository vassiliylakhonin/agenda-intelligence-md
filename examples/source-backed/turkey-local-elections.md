# Turkey local election results

**Signal classification:** weak_signal
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

Opposition gains in Turkey local elections signal potential policy shift risk for investors with Turkish exposure.

## What changed

Opposition party won mayoralties in major cities including Istanbul and Ankara, reversing years of ruling party dominance in urban centers.

## Why it matters

Municipal control affects infrastructure procurement, local regulation, and signals broader political trajectory that investors and trade partners monitor.

## Main uncertainty

Local election results do not directly translate to national policy changes; ruling party retains parliamentary majority.
