# EU Digital Services Act enforcement

**Signal classification:** compliance_relevant_development
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

First DSA enforcement actions by the European Commission set precedent for platform obligations; non-EU platforms with EU users must comply.

## What changed

European Commission opened formal proceedings against major platforms for alleged non-compliance with DSA transparency and risk-assessment obligations.

## Why it matters

Platforms face fines up to 6% of global turnover; compliance teams need operational processes for risk assessments and content moderation transparency.

## Main uncertainty

Outcome of proceedings will define the practical enforcement standard; appeal timeline may delay precedent effect.

## Affected actors

- European Commission
- very large online platforms
- digital advertisers
- content moderation vendors

## Scenarios

**Precedent-setting fine:** Commission issues substantial fine, clarifying compliance bar.
- Fine decision published
- other platforms update compliance programs

**Negotiated compliance:** Platform agrees to remedial measures without fine.
- Commitment decision
- voluntary audit program announced

## Watch next

- Commission preliminary findings publication
- Platform compliance plan submissions
- First fine decisions under DSA
