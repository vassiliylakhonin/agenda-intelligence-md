# US-China tariff escalation

**Signal classification:** structural_shift
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

New US tariffs on Chinese goods across technology and industrial sectors signal a structural shift in trade policy, not a negotiating tactic.

## What changed

US Trade Representative published final tariff schedule covering semiconductors, EVs, batteries, steel, and aluminum with rates of 25-100%.

## Why it matters

Affects global supply chains, reshoring economics, and procurement strategies for any firm with China-dependent inputs.

## Main uncertainty

China's retaliatory measures have been announced but not fully implemented; scope of exemptions remains unclear.

## Affected actors

- US importers
- Chinese exporters
- ASEAN manufacturers
- European industrial buyers

## Scenarios

**Escalation spiral:** Tit-for-tat retaliation across additional sectors.
- China targets US agricultural exports
- WTO dispute filings

**Managed decoupling:** Both sides accept higher tariffs as new baseline; trade re-routes through third countries.
- Vietnam/Mexico import surge from China
- no further retaliation rounds

## Watch next

- China Ministry of Commerce retaliation list publication
- US exemption petition filings
- ASEAN FDI inflow data for tariff-circumvention reshoring
