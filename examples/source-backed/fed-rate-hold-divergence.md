# Federal Reserve rate hold amid ECB cuts

**Signal classification:** signal
**Evidence mode:** live_source_backed

## Bottom line

Fed holds rates while ECB cuts, widening the transatlantic rate differential and strengthening the dollar against the euro.

## What changed

FOMC held the federal funds rate unchanged at its latest meeting while the ECB proceeded with a 25bp cut.

## Why it matters

Rate divergence affects EUR/USD, dollar-denominated debt servicing costs, and capital flows to emerging markets.

## Main uncertainty

Fed forward guidance suggests data-dependent approach; next meeting could go either way.

## Affected actors

- Federal Reserve
- ECB
- EM central banks
- FX-exposed corporates
- dollar-denominated bond issuers

## Scenarios

**Convergence:** Fed cuts at next meeting, narrowing differential.
- Softer US inflation data
- Fed governor speeches signaling pivot

**Sustained divergence:** Fed holds through year-end; EUR/USD approaches parity.
- Sticky US inflation
- strong labor market data

## Watch next
