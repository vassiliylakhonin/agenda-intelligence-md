# Real‑World Brief Example: Hormuz Strait Shipping Risk (2026)

**Bottom line:** Rising US‑Iran tensions, Houthi drone attacks, and new “gray‑zone” maneuvers are pushing insurers to raise war‑risk premiums for tankers passing Hormuz, threatening a 15‑20% spike in regional oil‑product prices by Q4 2026.

**Signal classification:** structural shift

**What changed:**  
- US CENTCOM deployed additional Patriots and cyber‑units to Bahrain (May 2026).  
- Iran’s IRGC unveiled a new “swarm‑drone” doctrine that explicitly targets LNG carriers.  
- Saudi Aramco and ADNOC requested “enhanced escort” clauses in shipping contracts.  
- Lloyd’s market raised war‑risk premium for Hormuz transits by 0.75 % of hull value (effective 1 June 2026).

**Why it matters:**  
- Hormuz carries ~21 million bbl/day (≈21 % of global petroleum liquids). A 2‑week blockade would erase ~300 million bbl from inventories, forcing strategic releases.  
- Higher freight & insurance costs cascade into Asian LNG importers (Japan, South Korea) and European gas‑oil consumers.  
- The US – China “shadow‑insurance” competition intensifies, creating new compliance headaches for P&I clubs.

**Who is affected:**  
- National oil companies (Saudi Aramco, Kuwait Petroleum, QatarEnergy).  
- Shipping & logistics firms (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, regional feeder lines).  
- Insurers & reinsurers (Lloyd’s syndicates, Swiss Re, Munich Re).  
- Energy traders (Vitol, Trafigura, Glencore) and downstream refiners in Asia & Europe.

**Main uncertainty:**  
- Will Iran opt for calibrated “pin‑prick” harassment vs. an outright blockade?  
- Speed of US‑led naval escort scaling (Q3 2026 timeline).  
- Whether China will deploy its own escort for “neutral” tankers, altering the risk‑allocation model.

**Scenarios:**  
1. **Managed tension** – US‑Iran back‑channel holds, drone incidents stay below threshold, premiums stabilise at +1 %.  
2. **Escalation spiral** – a tanker is hit, Lloyd’s withdraws cover, Saudi/ADNOC cut output by 30 %, Brent spikes to $110‑$120.  
3. **Policy pivot** – G7 agrees on a “Hormuz‑Insurance‑Pool”, premiums fall back, but compliance costs for ship‑owners rise (KYC on Iranian beneficial owners).

**Watch next:**  
- First US‑escorted convoy announcement (expected June 2026).  
- Lloyd’s underwriting agency bulletin on premium revisions (monthly).  
- IRGC‑Navy drill announcements (typically 2‑week notice).  
- China’s Ministry of Transport “guidelines for overseas energy shipping” (Q3 2026).  
- Any Houthi claim of “successful” drone strike on a VLCC.
