# Example: Red-Team Brief

**Claim being tested:** This policy announcement marks a durable shift.

**Best argument for it:** The actor has moved beyond rhetoric and created an implementation mechanism.

**Best argument against it:** No budget, timeline, or enforcement authority has been confirmed.

**Hidden assumption:** The announcing actor has enough political capital to implement the measure.

**Weak evidence point:** Current evidence may reflect positioning before negotiation, not final policy.

**What would falsify it:** Delay, dilution in draft text, lack of agency guidance, or contradictory signals from coalition partners.

**Revised assessment:** Treat as a meaningful signal, but not a structural shift until implementation details appear.
